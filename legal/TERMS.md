## What SomniAI is

An alarm app that plans your night backwards from the time you must be up, tells you when a wake-up isn't reachable, rings in stages, and checks that you are awake before it stops. It can use data you connect (sleep from Health Connect, screen time, Google Calendar) to plan and predict, and it can alert a wake buddy or text an emergency contact you choose.

## What it is not

- **Not a medical device and not medical advice.** Sleep plans, predictions and reports are estimates for planning your day. They do not diagnose or treat anything. For a sleep problem, see a doctor.
- **Not a guaranteed wake-up.** SomniAI is built to ring without the network and to keep ringing, but no alarm can be guaranteed: a phone that is off, out of battery, muted by a setting SomniAI can't override, or stopped by the manufacturer's battery manager may not ring. The app's bedtime check tells you about the problems it can detect. Don't rely on SomniAI alone where missing a wake-up could harm you or others: set a second alarm, or ask someone.
- **Not an emergency service.** Alerts to a wake buddy and texts to an emergency contact are best effort, depend on phones, networks and other companies' services, and may be late or not arrive. If someone may be in danger, call the emergency services.
