# Delete your SomniAI account

You can delete your SomniAI account, and everything stored with it, at any time. You don't need the app.

## How

**On the web, or in the app:** [sign in](/sign-in?callbackUrl=/%23your-data), go to *Your data* at the bottom of the home page, and choose **Delete my account**. Type your email address (and your password, if you have one) to confirm. It happens at once.

**If you can't sign in** (you have no access to the account any more): write to {{CONTACT}} from the account's email address, asking to delete the SomniAI account. We confirm it is your account by replying to that address, then delete it within 30 days and tell you when it's done.

## What is deleted

Everything SomniAI stores about the account, from the live database, at once: your account and settings, alarms and what happened when they rang, tasks, sleep log (including nights read from Health Connect), screen-time minutes, morning reports, alarm telemetry and its consent, the study enrolment and everything collected for the study, buddy pairings and alert records, your phones' push addresses, and the Google Calendar connection (Google is asked to remove SomniAI's access). Deleting from the app also cancels and forgets the alarms on that phone.

## What isn't

- Texts and alerts already delivered to your wake buddy or emergency contact stay on their phones. Twilio and Google keep their own delivery records under their own policies.
- Counts of recent requests from your internet address, kept for security, are forgotten on their own within two hours.
- An anonymised study export a researcher already took can't be recalled; it never contained your name or email address.
